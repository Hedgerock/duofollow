Privacy Policy

Last updated on November 15, 2026

This policy covers the DuoFollow website and the DuoFollow Connector extension for Chrome. DuoFollow is an independent project maintained by Dmitro Vasyliev.

Data used for Duolingo features

When you grant access in the Connector settings, the extension reads the Duolingo session cookie on www.duolingo.com to identify the signed-in account and check the session. The cookie is used inside the extension and by the browser for authenticated requests to Duolingo. The DuoFollow page receives the connection status.

To compare follows, the Connector sends the username you enter to Duolingo. It uses account IDs, usernames, display names, avatar URLs and follow relationships from the responses. The Connector compares the lists in your browser and sends the results and relevant profile details to the DuoFollow page. If you confirm an unfollow, the Connector sends the account ID and selected profile ID to Duolingo to remove that follow.

Where the data goes

DuoFollow has no developer-operated account or results database. Duolingo receives the HTTPS requests needed for the features you use. The browser loads avatars directly from the image hosts listed in the Duolingo responses. Those hosts receive connection information such as your IP address.

The DuoFollow site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. DuoFollow uses the data described here only to provide its features and does not use it for advertising, sale or credit decisions. We handle user data in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and your choices

The Connector saves your consent in its local Chrome storage. The website saves your language, theme and the username of the last successful comparison in your browser. When you return and access is available, DuoFollow can repeat that comparison. Results are held in the page’s memory. The browser may cache avatar images.

To stop access, open DuoFollow Connector and choose “Disconnect”. The extension revokes its Duolingo permissions and the site clears the comparison and remembered username when it detects the change. You can also revoke site access in Chrome. Clear the DuoFollow site data in Chrome to remove the language, theme and saved username. Changes already made to follows remain in Duolingo.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete data under applicable law, email devdmitrovasyliev@gmail.com. If you contact us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond to your request.
